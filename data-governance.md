# Data governance and responsible use

## Before adding a record

- Confirm the narration and exact reference against a reliable primary or scholarly source.
- Record collection, edition, numbering convention, language, translator, and source URL.
- Preserve grading exactly as attributed by the cited authority; do not infer or generate a grade.
- Check copyright, database terms, and permission to redistribute both original text and translation.
- Seek qualified scholarly review for the intended use and context.

## Product safeguards

- Never generate a narration, reference, or authenticity grade when the dataset does not contain it.
- Display source and reference alongside every result.
- Distinguish narration, translation, and commentary.
- Treat similarity as topical relevance only, not proof of authenticity or religious applicability.
- Return no result rather than making unsupported claims.
- Do not market this prototype as a fatwa service or replacement for qualified scholars.

Potential starting points for source research include Sunnah.com and Dorar.net. Their presence here is not an endorsement of blanket reuse; review each source's reliability, terms, and permissions independently.
